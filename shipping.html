Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about how your order gets to you, and what to do if something isn't right.

Shipping

We currently deliver across Cairo and 6th of October City. Orders are handled through our Uber courier service, ensuring your items arrive safely and on time.

Delivery Areas Cairo & 6th of October City only
Delivery Time 2–3 business days from order confirmation
Delivery Fee Calculated at checkout based on the Uber courier rate to your location at the time of delivery
Order Tracking You will receive an update once your order is out for delivery
📍 We are currently only able to deliver within Cairo and 6th of October City. We hope to expand to more cities soon.

Returns

We want you to love what you ordered. If you're not satisfied for any reason, you can request a return within 7 days of receiving your order.

Return Window 7 days from the date of delivery
Refund Method Full refund to your original payment method
Return Shipping Contact us to arrange pickup — we'll coordinate the return courier

How to Return an Item

  • 1
    Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order via the Contact page or WhatsApp and let us know which item(s) you'd like to return.
  • 2
    We'll confirm your return request and arrange a courier pickup from your address.
  • 3
    Once the item is received and inspected, your refund will be processed promptly.
